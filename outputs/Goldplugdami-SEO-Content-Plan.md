# Goldplugdami SEO and Content Plan

**Version:** 0.1 — planning draft. Search visibility cannot be guaranteed.

## 1. Search intent and positioning

Serve people looking for gold jewelry and bars in Lagos, shoppers comparing Karat/weight/price, buyers seeking a physical store in Tejuosho/Yaba, and people who need delivery elsewhere in Nigeria. Content must describe actual products and service coverage; do not publish city pages without meaningful local service information.

## 2. Site structure proposal

```text
/
├── /shop/
├── /category/earrings/
├── /category/gold-rings/
├── /category/bangles/
├── /category/pendants/
├── /category/necklaces/
├── /gold-bars/
├── /gold-rates/ (if a useful, indexable rate history/explainer exists)
├── /gold-karat-guide/
├── /authenticity-and-receipts/
├── /delivery-and-payment/
├── /visit-us/
└── /contact/
```

Product detail URLs should be stable and descriptive. Avoid creating thin pages for each Karat or Nigerian state unless they have unique user value.

## 3. Content requirements

- Home page: gold jewelry and gold bars, Lagos base, nationwide delivery, browse/rate actions, current trust evidence.
- Category pages: original intro, useful product filters, unique product photos/data, stock state, internal links.
- Product pages: actual name, category, full Karat, weight, applicable rate timestamp, calculated NGN price, stock, image alt text, authenticity evidence, WhatsApp CTA.
- Karat guide: explain the Karat options Goldplugdami sells using accurate language reviewed by the business; do not make unsupported purity claims.
- Rates page/module: current owner-entered rate, timestamp, explanation of rate × weight calculation and price freshness.
- Authenticity/receipt page: explain what hallmark is present, what receipt contains, gold bar ID, and how a buyer verifies it once the process is known.
- Delivery/payment page: bank transfer before delivery, shop payment on arrival, nationwide delivery terms once fees/timing/returns are confirmed.
- Visit page: exact public address, hours, visit arrangement, contact, map/directions only after details verified.

## 4. Local SEO and Google presence

1. Confirm the physical customer-facing location and whether visitors can attend during stated hours or by appointment.
2. Create/claim Google Business Profile using the real business name, accurate address/service-area setup, phone, hours, and category. Follow current Google eligibility rules; do not show a private fulfillment address as a storefront.
3. Keep name, address, phone, hours, and URL consistent between website, profile, and eligible directories.
4. Add a map/directions link once exact public address is approved; do not invent a pin from “Tejuosho, Yaba” alone.
5. Ask real customers for honest reviews without incentives or fabricated review content.

## 5. On-page and technical SEO checklist

- Unique title and meta description per indexable page; useful H1 and descriptive headings.
- Canonical URL, HTTPS, XML sitemap, robots rules, clean redirects, crawlable links.
- Server-rendered or prerendered product/category facts so search engines see content without interaction.
- Descriptive image filenames and accurate alt text; compress and size responsive formats without changing product appearance.
- Breadcrumbs and internal links among category, product, rates, trust, delivery, and visit pages.
- Structured data only when it matches visible, current content. Product merchant listing enhancements are not guaranteed and generally expect an on-page purchase capability; review eligibility when checkout exists. Do not publish structured prices/stock that disagree with the page.
- Connect Google Search Console and analytics with privacy-aware event measurement.

## 6. Keyword and content research

Seed research with real customer language around “gold jewelry Lagos,” “gold shop Yaba,” “gold earrings Lagos,” “gold ring Tejuosho,” “gold bangles Nigeria,” “gold chain Nigeria,” and “gold bar Nigeria.” Validate search demand and wording with Search Console, Google autocomplete/trends where available, and actual WhatsApp questions. Do not imply guaranteed rankings or fabricate search volume.

## 7. Conversion content and trust

Each product page should answer: What is it? What Karat? How much does it weigh? How is its price calculated? Is it available? What proof is provided? How do I ask/order? Where is the shop? How does delivery/payment work? Keep claims precise and place proof near the decision CTA.

## 8. Measurement

Track page view, category selection, product detail view, rate interaction, WhatsApp click by SKU/category, directions click, and sold-out view. Do not put names, phone numbers, or message contents into analytics. Review Search Console impressions/clicks, qualified WhatsApp inquiries, and stock/price mismatch incidents monthly.

## 9. Open inputs

WhatsApp number; approved public address and hours; customer authenticity verification instructions; product inventory/images; delivery fees/windows/returns; final page/URL choice; analytics/privacy consent requirements.
