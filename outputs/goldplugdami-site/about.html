ABOUT GOLDPLUGDAMI

Gold from Lagos, with clarity.

Goldplugdami is a Lagos gold trader offering gold jewelry and gold bars in 18 Karat, 21 Karat, 22 Karat, and 24 Karat.

Explore earrings, rings, bangles, pendants, necklaces, and bracelets. Each piece is listed with its Karat and weight in grams. Its price is calculated from that Karat’s daily selling rate per gram and the item’s weight.

Customers can enquire online or arrange an in-person visit. Delivery is available from Lagos to locations across Nigeria.
