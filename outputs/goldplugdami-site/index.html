QUICK ANSWERS

Gold, explained.

Clear details about rates, weights, payment, and delivery.

How do you calculate the final price?

We multiply the item’s weight in grams by that day’s selling rate per gram for its Karat. Ask us to confirm the final Naira amount before paying.

Can I confirm the exact Karat, weight, and stock first?

Yes. Each product listing shows its Karat and weight in grams. Message us with the item to confirm it is still available and verify today’s rate.

Which Karat options can I buy?

Goldplugdami offers 18 Karat, 21 Karat, 22 Karat, and 24 Karat gold. Availability depends on the individual item.

How much is delivery, and when will my order arrive?

Delivery cost and timing depend on your destination. Message us with your location for a delivery quote and estimated arrival time before payment.

How do online and in-person payments work?

Online orders are paid for by bank transfer before delivery. If you visit the shop, you can pay in person.

Where can I view items in person?

Visit the shop at Tejuosho, Yaba, Lagos. Contact us first to arrange your visit.
