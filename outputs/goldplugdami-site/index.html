QUICK ANSWERS

Gold, explained.

Clear details about rates, weights, payment, and delivery.

How is the price of a gold piece calculated?

We multiply the item’s weight in grams by that day’s selling rate per gram for its Karat. Rates can change daily, so confirm the final Naira price with us before paying.

Can I confirm an item’s Karat, weight, and stock before ordering?

Yes. Each product listing shows its Karat and weight in grams. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm that the item is still available and check its current rate.

Which Karat options are available?

Goldplugdami offers 18 Karat, 21 Karat, 22 Karat, and 24 Karat gold. The Karat available depends on the individual piece.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

We deliver from Lagos to locations across Nigeria. Share your destination so we can confirm delivery options, cost, and estimated timing before payment.

How can I pay for an online order or a shop visit?

Online orders are paid for by bank transfer before delivery. If you visit the shop, you can pay in person. Website checkout is planned for a future update.

What gold details are recorded on my receipt?

Your purchase receipt records the item’s hallmark number. Gold bars also have an authenticity ID code marked on the bar; ask us to confirm those details before purchase.

Where can I visit Goldplugdami?

Goldplugdami is based at Tejuosho, Yaba, Lagos. Contact us to confirm visit details before setting out.
